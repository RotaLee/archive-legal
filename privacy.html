隱私政策Privacy Policy

最後更新:2026 年 9 月 15 日Last updated: September 15, 2026

Archive 保存的資料Information Archive Stores

Archive 會在你的裝置上保存你主動輸入的語言偏好、收藏名稱、照片、地點、價格、故事、標籤、願望清單、收納位置、保養任務與提醒設定。Archive 沒有帳號系統，不會要求也不會保存電子郵件或密碼。Archive stores the language preference, collection names, photos, locations, prices, stories, tags, wishlist entries, storage locations, maintenance tasks, and reminder settings you choose to provide — on your device. Archive has no accounts and never asks for or stores an email address or password.

資料存在哪裡Where Your Data Lives

你的收藏存在這台裝置上。Premium 使用者的資料會另外備份到你自己的 iCloud 私有資料庫——那是你 Apple ID 底下的空間，開發者看不到也取不到其中的內容。Premium 使用者可以隨時匯出一份包含照片的備份檔自行保存；從備份檔恢復不需要訂閱。Archive 沒有自己的伺服器，不會把你的收藏傳送到任何第三方。Your collection lives on this device. For Premium users it is also backed up to your own private iCloud database — space under your Apple ID that the developer cannot see or retrieve. Premium users can export a backup file including photos at any time and keep it themselves; restoring from a backup file never requires a subscription. Archive runs no server of its own and does not send your collection to any third party.

資料用途How We Use Information

Archive 使用資料來顯示收藏、建立貼紙與展示卡、整理地圖與月曆、提供願望清單、收納 QR Code、保養提醒、備份與訂閱權限。Archive 不會出售你的個人資料,也不會為了跨 App 追蹤而蒐集資料。Archive uses data to display collections, create stickers and display cards, organize maps and calendars, provide wishlists, storage QR codes, maintenance reminders, backups, and subscription access. Archive does not sell your personal information and does not collect data for cross-app tracking.

照片與檔案Photos and Files

你加入的照片與匯出的圖片只會用於你選擇使用的功能,例如收藏展示、貼紙、分享卡與備份。若功能需要系統相機、相簿或雲端服務,Archive 只會在功能所需範圍內使用。Photos and exported images are used only for features you choose, such as collection display, stickers, share cards, and backups. When camera, photo library, or cloud services are needed, Archive uses them only as required for the feature.

通知Notifications

若你允許通知,Archive 可能會發送保養提醒或收藏相關提醒。你可以在 iOS 系統設定中調整通知權限。If you allow notifications, Archive may send maintenance reminders or collection-related reminders. You can change notification permissions in iOS Settings.

第三方服務Third-Party Services

Archive 只使用 Apple 自家的服務:App Store 內購(訂閱),以及 iCloud（CloudKit 私有資料庫，用於備份）。Archive 不使用任何第三方分析、廣告或後端服務。Archive uses only Apple's own services: App Store in-app purchases for subscriptions, and iCloud (CloudKit private database) for backup. Archive uses no third-party analytics, advertising, or backend services.

資料保留與刪除Retention and Deletion

刪除 App 就會移除這台裝置上的所有收藏資料。iCloud 上的備份可以在 App 內「設定 → 雲端資料 → 刪除 iCloud 上的備份」自行移除。因為沒有帳號、也沒有開發者持有的伺服器，Archive 這邊沒有任何你的資料需要保留或刪除。Premium 使用者建議定期匯出備份檔自行保存。Deleting the app removes every treasure stored on this device. The iCloud backup can be removed in the app under Settings → iCloud Data → "Delete iCloud Backup". Because there are no accounts and no developer-held server, there is no data on our side to retain or delete. For Premium users, exporting a backup file from time to time is recommended.

安全Security

Archive 會採取合理的技術與管理措施保護資料,但任何 App、裝置或網路服務都無法保證絕對安全。Archive uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no app, device, or internet service can be guaranteed to be perfectly secure.

兒童與監護Children and Guardians

Archive 適合能依法使用 App 的使用者。未成年人使用時,應由家長或監護人了解並同意。Archive is intended for users who can lawfully use apps. If a minor uses Archive, a parent or guardian should review and consent to use.
